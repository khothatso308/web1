CUSTOM ORDERS

At Cathy’s Cakes, we believe that every celebration is unique, and your cake should be just as special as the occasion you are celebrating. While our website showcases a selection of our cake designs to give you inspiration and help you explore the different styles, flavours, themes, and ideas we offer, you are not limited to ordering only what is displayed on our website. We are happy to create completely customised cakes based on your personal preferences, including your preferred colours, flavours, sizes, shapes, themes, decorations, characters, messages, and overall design. Whether you have a specific cake idea in mind, want to recreate a design you have seen elsewhere, or simply have a rough idea that you would like us to turn into something beautiful, our team is here to work with you and bring your vision to life. Custom orders are perfect for birthdays, weddings, baby showers, graduations, anniversaries, corporate events, themed parties, and any other special occasion where you want something personal and memorable. You can also request special combinations of flavours, fillings, toppings, decorations, and colours to make your cake truly your own. Simply contact us with your ideas, inspiration pictures, preferred date, number of guests, and any other requirements, and we will discuss the available options with you and provide a suitable quote. If you can imagine it, we can work with you to create it, making sure your cake is not only delicious but also designed specifically to match your celebration and personal style.
